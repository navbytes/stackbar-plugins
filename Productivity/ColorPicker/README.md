# Color Picker

Pick a color from anywhere on screen and copy it. Run **Pick Color** from the palette (or press the button in the panel), click any pixel with the magnifier, and the color is on your clipboard as hex, RGB or HSL.

**Picked Colors** lists the last 30 colors you picked. Return copies a color in your chosen format, and ⌘1, ⌘2 and ⌘3 copy it as hex, RGB or HSL.

## What it needs

Nothing. The magnifier is macOS's own, and Stackbar 0.26.0 or later.

## Settings

- **Copy as:** hex (`#0A60C8`), RGB (`rgb(10, 96, 200)`) or HSL (`hsl(213, 90%, 41%)`).
- **Clear Picked Colors** empties the list.
